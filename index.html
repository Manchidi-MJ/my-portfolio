About

I'm Magaseng John Manchidi, a South African Computer Science Honours graduate (University of Limpopo, 2025) working across two tracks: security & networking and data & analytics. My academic journey began with a BSc in Mathematical Sciences and continued through BSc Honours in Computer Science.

Research

Research & publications

Honours Thesis · 2025

Evaluating Machine Learning Techniques for Adaptive Ransomware Detection

Supervisors: Dr S.S. Mapunya, Prof M. Velempini University of Limpopo

A comparative evaluation of supervised and unsupervised machine-learning models for ransomware detection using public datasets (Malimg, Microsoft BIG 2015). The study examined ensemble methods — reporting a best Random Forest accuracy of 99.34% on the evaluated data — and discussed feature importance and adaptability toward previously unseen variants.
